If you double the voltage in a circuit while keeping the resistance constant, what happens to the electric current and why?
Pedagogical Guiding Hint
Look at the mathematical formula V = I * R. What must happen to I if V increases and R remains fixed?
Verified Model Explanation
According to Ohm's law, I = V / R. Current is directly proportional to voltage. Therefore, doubling the voltage doubles the current flowing through the circuit.
